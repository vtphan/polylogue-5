## Authorial notes

Episode 7 is Priya's episode. Her inflection is the center of the
scene and the other three characters' moves are shaped around it —
Maya's instant *oh, that's what I was trying to say*, Cam's honest
re-seeing, Jules's tired tip-into-binary when the data refuses to
confirm anything. That is what `distributed_self_correction` looks
like when the carrier of the missing tool finally arrives: the
whole group moves, but they move because one person has named the
thing the whole group was stuck on.

On Priya as the sole strength carrier for the inflection: the design
doc is explicit — *Priya is the one who names the sampling bias out
loud* — and the pedagogical payoff of the whole story's scope arc
depends on this line belonging to her. Any dilution (having Maya or
Cam half-name it first, or having Priya only confirm what someone
else said) would blow the inflection. The dialog writer should
stage Priya's line as the shortest turn in the scene, without
introduction or qualifier, and should let the other three characters
do the dramatic reacting. Priya herself should not register the
moment as a moment.

On the two Priya strengths in one episode: this is dense but
deliberate. The first strength (`condition_sensitivity`) reverses
her ep-4 weakness and closes the facet's arc; the second
(`perspective_engagement`) plants a new facet that she will carry
across the back half of the season. Loading both into ep 7 lets the
episode do two kinds of scope work — auditing the conditions of
existing data (map) and widening the frame to notice what is missing
(habitat, city, cut-off-ness). The second move is also the seed for
ep 9's mundane genetic answer (habitat island + isolation +
founder-effect preserved allele frequencies), and planting it in
ep 7 rather than in ep 9 gives the ep-9 chain something already on
the notebook page to build from.

On `perspective_engagement` being the second strength facet (not
`condition_sensitivity` again): the move is about *what is missing
from the frame* (the rest of the city's trees, the Memphis Priya
hasn't walked) rather than about *the conditions under which the
data was collected*. Condition_sensitivity is a within-frame move;
perspective_engagement is a frame-widening move. The story's
declared facet inventory includes both on Priya, and ep 7 is the
natural home for perspective_engagement's first planting because
the physical setting (the park's edge) makes the frame-widening
move concrete and visible.

On Cam's small strength beat: this is not an inflection — ep 8 is
Cam's inflection. What ep 7 does for Cam is show him applying a
conditions-of-data audit to his own reading for the first time,
with Priya having named the tool he can now use. The move must
stay small to preserve ep 8's weight. One short line (*I locked
in*), a nod, and then Cam is just a walking member of the north-
side expedition. Reviewers should feel this as a kid learning a
move in real time, not as the growth beat.

On Maya without a target in ep 7: Maya has carried strength in
five of six prior episodes and her next planned inflection is ep 9.
Giving her a target in ep 7 would over-load her arc and crowd
Priya's episode. The *oh, that's what I was trying to say
yesterday* line in beat 2 is her present-and-reacting move, not a
designed target — it is her half-sentence from ep 6 retroactively
validated, and it is beat-level, not target-level. The reader
should feel the continuity (Maya was almost there yesterday; Priya
closed the gap today) without the episode having to formalize it.

On Jules's weakness carrying forward: Jules has no growth arc, and
her binary-framing pattern must stay live across the season for
ep 10's acceptance-not-growth to land. Ep 7's ambiguous-glimpse-
tipping-into-binary is the same pattern in yet another register.
The move should be small and should resolve without confrontation
— Jules is tired, the group pushes back gently, the scene moves on.
This is not a setback or a moral failing; it is her engine still
running.

No social dynamics. The scene is cooperative, the corrections are
honest, and no one is softening an objection because a friend is
scared — Jules's fear has thinned considerably by ep 7, and Priya's
naming of the sampling bias does not make anyone fragile. The
`group_pressure` texture will return in ep 8 when Jules is rattled
by what Cam finds out about Anya; ep 7 runs without that layer.

## Why these targets

**inferential_validity / Jules / black_and_white_thinking.** The
design doc commits Jules to this pairing across the season, and
ep 7 is the natural home for one more instance of it — the
ambiguous glimpse on the walk is exactly the kind of data Jules's
frame cannot hold as ambiguous. This is not a new planting; it is
a continuing demonstration of the pattern, which her engine-role
requires to stay active. Episode 2 applied the pattern to framing
of a question (*aliens or boring*); ep 5 applied it to the closing
chain (*genetics AND aliens*); ep 7 applies it to an observation
that is genuinely uncertain. Three different surfaces, same habit.

**condition_sensitivity (strength) / Priya.** The design doc names
this as Priya's inflection and specifies the line: *we were drawing
a map of the part of the park we walk.* The move is a full
reversal of her ep-4 weakness on the same facet, and the facet's
weakness-to-strength arc runs from ep 4 through the ep-6 half-
naming (Maya) to ep 7's clean naming (Priya). This is the
pedagogical payoff of the whole scope arc, and the planting is
doctrinal.

**perspective_engagement (strength) / Priya.** The design doc
declares this facet and assigns it to Priya as a growing strength
in the back half. Episode 7 is the facet's first appearance and
the seed for ep 9's mundane answer. Planting it here rather than
later gives the habitat-island observation time to sit in the
notebook before ep 9 builds the chain from it. The facet is
distinct from condition_sensitivity — a frame-widening move rather
than a within-frame audit — and the story benefits from showing
both kinds of scope work in the same episode, carried by the same
character, to illustrate that scope is a *family of moves* rather
than one move.

**condition_sensitivity (strength) / Cam.** Cam's ep-6 weakness
on this facet turns here in small proto form. The planting is
not an inflection (ep 8 is the inflection); it is a between-scene
beat that shows Cam can do the move with help. Putting the beat
here rather than deferring it to ep 8 gives ep 8's self-initiated
Anya call more room — by ep 8 Cam will already have applied one
conditions-of-data audit in ep 7 (on the map) and can apply
another (on the ep-3 post replies) without the audit itself being
the full inflection. Ep 8's inflection is the *self-initiated
call to a credentialed adult*, which builds on the audit habit
planted across eps 2 (grudging TikTok check), 5 (peer-reading
Maya's page), and 7 (reseeing his own map-reading).

## Calibration note for this episode

The register opens up and quiets down at the same time. The walk
itself — four kids crossing a road in their own park to a path
none of them has walked — is physically new and narratively
released; the reader should feel the freshness of being somewhere
the investigation has not been. But the moves inside the scene
are all quiet: Priya's naming is one short sentence; Cam's
correction is five words; Jules's tip-into-binary resolves without
confrontation; the edge-of-park observation goes in the notebook
without climax. The dialog writer should render Priya as flat-
affect in this scene — the inflection lives in what she says, not
in how she says it. Her delivery should be the delivery of a kid
noticing a thing, not the delivery of a kid making a point. Maya's
*oh* in beat 2 is the loudest single emotional moment in the
scene; Cam's honest *I locked in* is the second loudest. Priya
never dramatizes. That flatness is a design choice — her weakness
in ep 4 was a sentence that sounded like observation and landed as
conclusion, and her strength here is the same kind of sentence
playing the role her weakness played, but correctly. Same texture,
different valence. The uncanny calibration is almost entirely
dormant in this episode; Jules is tired rather than afraid.
