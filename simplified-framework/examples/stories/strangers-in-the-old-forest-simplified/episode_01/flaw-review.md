## Candidate Flaw Turns

Likely flaw:

- `jumping_to_a_conclusion`

Why it works:

- Jules moves directly from "the eyes looked red" to "something had to happen to it"
- the missing step is easy to name
- the flaw is in the wording of the turn itself, not hidden in background context

Why a 6th grader could pick it out:

- this can support a clean question like: "What is the problem with this reasoning?"
